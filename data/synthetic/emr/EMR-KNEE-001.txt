EMR-KNEE-001: Clinical note — right knee pain

History: 54-year-old patient with right knee pain for 3 months, worse with stairs and walking.
Treatment tried: 8 weeks of physical therapy and ibuprofen with no improvement.
Imaging: X-ray shows medial joint space narrowing and a possible meniscal tear.
Diagnosis: medial meniscal tear (ICD-10 M23.31).
