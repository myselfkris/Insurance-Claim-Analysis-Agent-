POL-KNEE-MRI: Knee MRI (CPT 73721)

Criteria for coverage:
1. The patient has knee pain that limits daily activities.
2. The patient tried at least six weeks of conservative care such as physical therapy or anti-inflammatory medicine.
3. An X-ray was done and shows a possible meniscal tear or joint space narrowing.

Knee MRI is covered when all three criteria are met.
